The problem

Dental reports are written in English and packed with clinical words. Many patients in India think in another language, and even English readers rarely know what "caries" or "gingivitis" means. They leave the clinic unsure what is wrong or what to do next, and treatment gets delayed.

How it works

  1. Paste the reportThe patient pastes the text of their dental report and picks a language.
  2. Explain it simplySarvam's language model rewrites it in plain words, with no jargon, and ends with one clear next step.
  3. Hear itSarvam's Bulbul voice reads the explanation aloud, so reading is not required.

Why it helps

  • Patients understand their condition in the language they think in.
  • Voice works for elderly patients and people who find reading hard.
  • Every explanation ends with an action, like booking a visit.
  • Dentists spend less chair time repeating the same explanations.

What comes next

  • Photo upload, so printed and handwritten reports can be read directly.
  • A link to X-ray analysis, so radiograph findings flow straight into plain-language explanations.
  • A dentist review step before anything reaches the patient.
